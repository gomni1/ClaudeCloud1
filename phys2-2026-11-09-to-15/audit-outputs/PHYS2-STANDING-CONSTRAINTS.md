## Settled — do not re-derive

- **There was no selection rule before A3, by design.** Observed Permissibility is a retention rule for candidate support architectures. It explicitly does not establish uniqueness, inevitability, completeness, numerical correctness or spontaneous formation. — P2.7-03 §6
- **Ladder principle.** Complete organisations at rung R(n−1) become causal resources from which complete organisations at rung R(n) *may* form. Lower entities are not incomplete pieces of higher ones; their identity and transformation repertoire are valid at their own level. Upper properties need not exist below in miniature. — P2.7-03 §§1–3
- **Identity is an invariant of the parallel functional organisation.** No support locus owns the particle. Propagation is displaced reorganisation of the whole functional allocation. The sequential recruit/transfer/certify/release picture, and the identity-token questions it generates, were rejected. — P2.6-03 §1
- **Perfect sameness cannot seed difference.** Total relational equivalence is insufficient as a primitive substrate.
- **Persistence is identity through replacement** — pattern-first, not carrier-first. A persistent structure is a relational signature maintained through admissible turnover, not a fixed primitive set or edge set.
- **Shared supply is permitted within aggregate capacity, with each allocated unit counted once.** — [P3A-09-Renewal-Posit-RA2-v01.md](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1R6v53iw8GY2uKLI-ATfAIBOR02JWWheb/view?usp=drivesdk) (file ID `1R6v53iw8GY2uKLI-ATfAIBOR02JWWheb`)
- **A3's occupancy reading measures concurrent response activity, not substrate.** — A3 selection record and occupancy assessment. *Basis (25 September):* the first half is Little's law at steady state (occupancy assessment). The second half follows from the identity and persistence lines above: carriers turn over and no support locus owns the organisation, so a count of substrate at an instant is not an invariant of anything and cannot be what A3 ranks. Settled by derivation, not by decision alone.
- **Boundary data does not fix interior structure.** Three related results, differently scoped — not one result found three times:
  - *Empirical, different graphs.* Three non-isomorphic countermodels remained compatible with every frozen signature. — P2.9B-05
  - *Fixed graph.* On one incidence graph, two flows with equal vertex balances differ exactly by a circulation; the circulation space has dimension m−n+1. — P3A-11; [P3-A3-Selection-Decision-and-Revisions.md](https://drive.google.com/file/d/13Qx0mTuFelYYmUErWM2gqr6qQEfhibRo/view?usp=drivesdk) §2
  - *Representation.* Independent relation-phase states cannot be represented by one scalar per participant; the loop coordinates are dual to that cycle space. — P3A-10C

  Equal boundary behaviour of *different* graphs does not make their difference a circulation. The P2.9B-05 countermodels are related to the fixed-graph theorem, not an instance of it. Their cycle ranks are register-dependent: 1, 0, 1 for collapsed skeletons; 1, 3, 4 for independently registered directed arcs. — A3 selection record §2 and table
- **Cross-scale reduction is lossy.** The compact trans-scalar grammar is retired. — [P3A-09-Renewal-Posit-RA2-v01.md](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1R6v53iw8GY2uKLI-ATfAIBOR02JWWheb/view?usp=drivesdk) (file ID `1R6v53iw8GY2uKLI-ATfAIBOR02JWWheb`)
- **Formation and maintenance are different regimes** with different feasibility conditions. — [P3A-09-Renewal-Posit-RA2-v01.md](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1R6v53iw8GY2uKLI-ATfAIBOR02JWWheb/view?usp=drivesdk) (file ID `1R6v53iw8GY2uKLI-ATfAIBOR02JWWheb`)
- **Window feasibility implies affordability** for fixed-delay divisible-route systems with downward-closed capacity constraints; affordability is redundant after feasibility in that class. Formation remains a separate dynamical question. — formation specification pass

## Forbidden moves

- **Do not descend a rung** unless a specific physical result requires a particular lower mechanism. Descent must be targeted, consequence-led and finite; return to the active rung once the load-bearing permission is recovered. — P2.7-03 §8. *The RA1-S1 descent violated this. It was triggered by a noticed specification gap, not by a result that demanded a lower mechanism.*
- **Observed Permissibility may not** excuse a contradiction, replace a missing mechanism with a slogan, assign particle identities by resemblance, install unrestricted lower variables, claim empirical recovery from qualitative compatibility, or declare a candidate unique because it is possible. — P2.7-03 §9
- **No claim of uniqueness or inevitability** when licensing an unresolved mechanism. — P2.7-03 §5 cl.7
- **No rescue.** Open mechanisms cannot be invoked after the fact to save a failed prediction. Changing the cost function or the constraints after a failure creates a new version, never a rescued prediction.
- **Do not stop at a calculation that merely certifies its own construction.** — P2.6-03 §3
- **A ledger posted by the same operation it audits is not independent conservation evidence.** — P2.6-03 §2
- **Passing arbitrary longevity, width, replacement or fidelity thresholds does not establish a particle.** — P2.6-03 §2
- **Do not invent a shape, state alphabet or numerical gate** merely to make the next phase executable. — P2.6-03, final method rule
- **No parameter tuning.** Kill conditions are preregistered before runs. Operating windows are discovered, not manufactured. Test infrastructure is itself tested with planted failures.
- **Anti-fitting.** A candidate may not be tuned to an observation and then credited with it. Observation may guide the search: where to look, what shape a gap has, and which known behaviours to take apart for their rules. — Glenn's decision, 5 October 2026; revised project instructions §6
- **Do not treat a process as a one-to-one handoff with single-unit bookkeeping.** A process is supported by vast numbers of substrate components and is treated as ensemble behaviour. — Glenn's decision, 5 October 2026; consistent with P2.6-03 §1. *The 5 October order test (repository gomni1/ClaudeCloud1) violated this: it was specified in a one-unit handoff grammar, and its framing was rejected.*
- **A model's own accounting is never a requirement on the mechanism.** A small model can audit every event; the substrate is not asked to. The handoff is statistical: the system stays within bounds and the number of participating elements tends toward the statistical basin. A particle is roughly 10^20 primitives across nested structures, and precise bookkeeping of each is no part of the picture. Event-by-event checks remain usable for catching errors inside a model. — Glenn's decision, 6–7 October 2026. *The September section audits' "complete per-event ledger" gates, and the 11 September synthesis that joined them into one build, violated this; that build is retired as framed.*
- **No coordinator above a level.** Nothing sits above a level making decisions for its organisations. What keeps them in step can only come from process interactions with a natural tendency to settle into stable resonance (or not): a behaviour co-emergent across the scale. A shared variable that the units of a level copy or follow is a coordinator by another name. — Glenn's statement, 2 November 2026. *The 10 November timing–extent run violated this: its E3 ensemble candidate (Claude's specification) had every primitive copy one shared organisation state, and its ρ > 0 results rest on that variable.*
- **The word "canon" is not used** — say not final / not uniquely derived / not promoted.
- **Terminology changes require Glenn's explicit checkpoint** before enactment.
- **No particle-register language in mechanical text.** Observational-register comparisons are labelled as such.

- **Is any addition mandatory?** — it comes with proof: something has been demonstrated or refuted that this file must now record.
- **Is any addition necessary?** — it comes with a requirement: the work cannot proceed correctly without it.

If neither, nothing is added. Also check that no existing line has become wrong or broader than its source.
